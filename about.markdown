#### fullproof for mornings (ffm) is a space for following along and reflecting.

#### In today's day and age, to find solace and quiet can feel like a chore. ffm is set up to be fool-proof, no non-sense, clear guidelines for adding calm and structure into your daily life.

#### Whether it’s an outdoor run, a 30-minute yoga flow, or a 15-minute meditation, ffm is yours and for you.

#### Pause and reflect with three simple prompts at the end of the day.

#### ffm is a tool—use it-a reliable way to bring structure and reflection to your daily life, however that may look for you.
