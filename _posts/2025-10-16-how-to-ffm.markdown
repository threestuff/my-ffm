How to FFM

1. Start practice or a few
2. Select from one of our selected videos or choose your own
3. Follow along and practice consistently (for success)
4. Journal and document your exercises/activities eg. 10 min yoga flow
5. Reflect on your day with three prompts
6. Repeat daily
